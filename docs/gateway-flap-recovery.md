# Paper Gateway flap: operator checklist

Use this when JoeDesk reports a late Gateway heartbeat, a Gateway outage, or stopped board updates. The board is a consumer of a paper snapshot; it does not reconnect IB or restart the pusher.

1. **Read the light and time.** Yellow means a signal is delayed or degraded; last known numbers may still be visible. Red means a serious problem such as a reported Gateway outage or board updates stopped for more than three freshness windows. Treat displayed money as a last observation, not a live broker view.
2. **Reload JoeDesk once.** If it returns green and the snapshot age and Gateway check-in age are current, continue watching it. Repeated reloads do not repair a Gateway or producer failure.
3. **Separate the failure.** “Gateway down/degraded” is a producer report about IB. “Gateway has not checked in” means its last heartbeat aged out; current connection state is unconfirmed. “Board updates stopped” or a failed refresh means the browser is not receiving current snapshots. Check the protected `/joe/data.json` `generatedAt`, `safety.gateway.status`, and `safety.gateway.lastSeenAt` through the normal SSO route; do not share the full payload publicly.
4. **Tell Amy/Markus** the light, reason, snapshot age, Gateway check-in age, whether a reload helped, and whether this is during New York regular trading hours. For a reported Gateway outage or persistent late heartbeat, Amy/Markus should inspect the hsb0 paper Gateway and the `ib-gateway-session.service` supervisor journal. For fresh Gateway heartbeats but stale snapshots, inspect the producer/pusher path instead. The supervisor service is a check and alert path, not the Gateway itself.
5. **Recovery is an operator decision.** Amy/Markus should confirm the paper account and current broker session before following the established Gateway restart or relogin procedure. Do not restart a unit by guessing its name, switch to live credentials, change HALT, or re-arm trading crons from this board. If flatten or click routines are broken, report their separate harness status to Amy; the current JoeDesk snapshot does not carry those fields.
6. **Verify the return.** Confirm new `generatedAt` and `lastSeenAt` values advance through more than one refresh, the Gateway status is `ok`, and the traffic light reflects current data. If the light stays yellow/red, keep the incident open and tell Amy what failed.

JoeDesk reads `/joe/data.json` every 15 seconds. Its freshness threshold comes from `safety.staleAfterSeconds`; a failed fetch is yellow while the last snapshot is recent, and a snapshot older than three thresholds is red. This is a display rule, not an automatic Gateway action.
